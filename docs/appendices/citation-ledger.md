## 1. Statutory & Regulatory Mining Standards

1. **Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS) Annual Reports (2014 – 2024):**
   * *Citation:* Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. Standard accident statistics across underground coal operations.
   * *Claim Verified:* Ground and roof fall accidents accounted for 63.4% of total underground coal mining fatalities (173 out of 273 fatalities over the recorded decadal analysis).
2. **Coal Mines Regulations (CMR), 2017:**
   * *Citation:* Notification G.S.R. 1461(E), Ministry of Coal, Government of India, 27 November 2017.
   * *Specific Standards:* 
     * **Regulation 111:** Strata Control and Monitoring Plan (SCAMP) mandating continuous instrumentation of extraction faces and depillaring zones.
     * **Regulation 112:** Precautions against ground movement and water inrushes from overlying aquifers.
3. **DGMS Circular No. 7 of 1997 (Tech):**
   * *Citation:* Directorate General of Mines Safety, Dhanbad, 29 August 1997.
   * *Specific Standards:* Permissible Peak Particle Velocity (PPV) thresholds for industrial structures and surface buildings overlying coal workings ($5.0\text{ to }15.0\text{ mm/s}$ based on dominant frequency).

## 2. Telecommunications & Spectrum Law

4. **Gazette of India Notification GSR 564(E):**
   * *Citation:* Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (Wireless Planning and Coordination Wing), New Delhi, 30 July 2008.
   * *Statutory Limits:* Delicenses the 865.000 – 867.000 MHz frequency band for low-power indoor and outdoor telemetry. Strictly mandates carrier bandwidth $\le 200\text{ kHz}$ and maximum conducted power $\le 1\text{ Watt}$ (30 dBm).

## 3. Geotechnical Mechanics & Subsidence Engineering

5. **Knothe, St. (1957):**
   * *Citation:* Knothe, St., *"Observations of surface movements under influence of mining and their theoretical interpretation"*, Proceedings of the European Congress on Ground Movement, University of Leeds, Leeds, UK, pp. 210–218.
   * *Equations Verified:* Gaussian influence function, spatial integration error functions, and time-dependent exponential relaxation factor $\eta(t) = 1 - e^{-ct}$.
6. **Awershin, S.G. (1947):**
   * *Citation:* Awershin, S.G., *"Ground Movement Under the Influence of Mining Operations"*, Ugletekhizdat, Moscow.
   * *Equations Verified:* Awershin's horizontal displacement relation $U_x = B_{\text{horiz}} \cdot (\partial S / \partial x)$ with $B_{\text{horiz}} \approx 0.32 \cdot r$.
7. **Burland, J.B., Broms, B.B., and de Mello, V.F.B. (1977):**
   * *Citation:* Burland, J.B. et al., *"Behaviour of foundations and structures"*, Building Research Establishment (BRE) Digest 251, Watford, UK.
   * *Standards Verified:* Tensile strain classification criteria: Category 0 to 5 damage thresholds (tensile cracking initiation at $\varepsilon \approx 1500\ \mu\varepsilon$).
